About the game

Full Court Deck is a roguelike deck builder in the spirit of Slay the Spire, played on a basketball court. Pick one of two parody stars, The King or The Chef, and play a single NBA-style season. Every game is a back-and-forth of offense and defense, and every play is a card.

Each month is a branching map of games, rivalry games, card shops, practice facilities, locker rooms and trade-deadline events, ending in a Marquee Game against a rival. Survive the regular season, then win three one-game playoff rounds and a best-of-three Finals against the Superteam.

How a game works

  • The opponent shows their next play before it happens, so you know what is coming.
  • Spend your Energy on scoring cards to add points, defense cards to cut down their next play, and playmaking cards to draw cards and build Momentum for one big shot.
  • First team to the target score wins. Lose a regular-season game and you lose Stamina equal to the margin of defeat. Run out of Stamina and your season is over.

Packs, serials and memorabilia

Wins and the Card Shop pay out in card packs. Cards come in numbered serials from Base up to /500, /250, /99, /25, /10 and the 1 of 1, and rarer serials are stronger. Watch for Showtime inserts, autographed cards and memorabilia like signed sneakers and game-worn jerseys that change how you play.

Your season, your Binder Collection and your Hall of Fame records save in your browser, so you can close the tab and pick up where you left off.
