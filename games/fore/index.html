About the game

Fore! is a golf game you play with one flick. You stand behind the ball on a driving range, flick to swing and watch the ball fly. Each shot takes a few seconds, and the fun is seeing how far it goes.

Fore! is in development. Right now it's a prototype of the swing on a plain range. Wind, different ground surfaces, game modes and upgrades come next.

How to swing

  • Press and hold anywhere. A ring starts shrinking toward the ball.
  • Flick up and let go as the ring meets the ball. Letting go right on time is a Perfect strike; early pulls the ball left and late pushes it right.
  • A faster flick hits it farther. Flick a little left or right to aim, and bend your flick to curve the ball the same way.
